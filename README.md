## 👋 About Me

I'm a **Platform Engineer and Cloud Architect** with **12+ years** of experience designing, automating and securing cloud-native platforms across **AWS, Azure and GCP**. I turn complex systems into resilient, scalable and cost-efficient platforms, and I've done it for startups, government programs and enterprise automotive and financial-services organizations.

My approach blends strategic architecture with hands-on execution: reusable patterns, golden paths and guardrails that make engineering teams **faster and safer**, with reliability and cost treated as first-class business metrics.

- 🔭 **Currently working on:** Platform Engineering for a US-based client
- 🤝 **Looking to collaborate with:** AI enthusiasts, Platform Engineering architects and Cloud Architecture specialists
- 🙋 **Looking for help with:** Applying LLMs and agents to day-to-day operations (alert triage, runbook automation, incident summarization), and sharing patterns on internal developer platforms at scale
- 🌱 **Currently learning:** AI-driven operations (AIOps) and LLM platform engineering, OpenTelemetry-native observability, internal developer platforms (Backstage), Crossplane and policy-as-code
- 💬 **Ask me about:** Cloud architecture and landing zones, Kubernetes platforms, Terraform/IaC, CI/CD and DevSecOps, SRE and observability, FinOps, and senior cloud interview preparation
- ⚡ **Fun fact:** I've moved workloads from physical servers to VMware ESXi to AWS, and now to GitOps-managed Kubernetes fleets. I still enjoy a good postmortem.
